Overview

OuiOui is a social application operated from Washington, United States. This Policy describes the categories of personal information we collect, the purposes for which we use it, the circumstances in which we disclose it, how long we retain it, and the choices available to you. We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

We may collect:

  • Your phone number for verification and account access.
  • Your display name, username, and date of birth.
  • Profile photos and other optional profile information you choose to provide.
  • Account settings, privacy settings, and notification preferences.

Content and social activity

We collect content and activity that you create or submit through OuiOui, including:

  • Photos and videos used in Moments or profiles, including audio contained in videos.
  • Moment audience selections, user tags, place tags, and related visibility choices.
  • Messages, conversation participation, reactions, likes, and other interactions.
  • Friend requests, friendships, lists, blocks, restrictions, votes, and reports.
  • Associated metadata, such as timestamps, participants, and content status.

Device and diagnostic information

We may automatically receive technical information needed to operate and protect the Service, such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version, a random installation identifier, push notification token, request timestamps, crash information, and performance diagnostics.

Camera, photo library, location, and notifications

With your permission, OuiOui may access your camera, photo library, location, and notification capabilities. You can manage these permissions through your device settings. Some features will not work without the permission they require.

Location and nearby features

OOuiOui uses a location sample to determine the city where you are using the app and provide city-based features. We may retain the resulting city or area and related freshness information, but we do not maintain a durable history of the precise coordinates used to determine it. Precise location is also used for certain optional nearby features as described below.

How precise location works

  • Appear Nearby is opt-in. When you turn on Appear Nearby, OuiOui temporarily associates your precise location with your account so eligible nearby friends can find you for tagging or conversation features.
  • Searching does not broadcast you. When you look for friends who are currently appearing nearby, OuiOui uses your current location for the proximity request, but this does not make you visible to other users.
  • Nearby place search is separate. OuiOui may use your current location to return nearby places that you can choose to tag in a Moment.
  • Precise location is temporary. Precise location used for these nearby features is held in short-lived server-side storage for no more than 90 seconds and is then automatically deleted. An Appear Nearby entry may be removed sooner when you stop appearing.

OuiOui does not show your precise coordinates to other users and does not maintain a durable history of your precise coordinates. We may retain a derived city or scene and related freshness information for city-based features. If you tag a person or place, or create a conversation using a nearby feature, the resulting tag, place selection, or conversation record may be retained according to its own lifecycle.

  • Apply the audience, privacy, block, restriction, and visibility choices used by the Service.
  • Provide city, nearby-user, nearby-place, and place-tagging features.
  • Store, process, deliver, and remove photos and videos.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and other harmful activity.
  • Investigate reports and enforce safety rules.
  • Diagnose errors, monitor reliability, and improve the Service.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data retention

We retain different categories of information for different periods, based on the feature, operational need, safety requirements, and applicable law. We may delete or anonymize information sooner when it is no longer needed.

Precise nearby location
Retained in a temporary cache for no more than 90 seconds.
City or scene context
May be retained longer than precise location to operate city-based features and validate current context.
Moments and media
Moments are designed to remain actively visible for up to three hours. Underlying records, references, and expired media may be retained longer where needed for media processing, notifications, conversations, safety, deletion, or technical operations. Expired Moment media may be retained for up to 14 days.
Messages and conversations
Messages and conversation records follow their own lifecycle and may remain after a related Moment is no longer visible. They may be removed sooner or made inaccessible because of deletion, blocking, reporting, or other safety actions.
Account and profile information
Generally retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, access is disabled and deletion of associated data begins, subject to limited safety, fraud, legal, operational, and backup retention.
Reports, security, and diagnostic records
May be retained for longer periods when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, prevent fraud, protect users, resolve disputes, maintain system security, or comply with law.

Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups until those backups rotate out of use. Backups are not restored for ordinary product use after a deletion request.

Your privacy choices and rights

  • You can manage location, camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can stop Appear Nearby from within OuiOui; the temporary location entry also expires automatically.
  • You can choose Moment audiences and use available privacy, list, restriction, block, and report controls.
  • You can update supported profile information from within the app.
  • You can delete your account from the account-management area in the app.
  • You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and appropriate identity verification.

Privacy rights vary by jurisdiction. We will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law.

Account deletion

You can delete your OuiOui account from the account-management area in the app. Deleting the app from your device does not by itself delete your account.

Account deletion disables access to the account and starts deletion of associated product data. We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for safety, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or backup rotation. Reports and other safety records may be retained even after related content or accounts are deleted.

You may also contact support@getouioui.com for help with a privacy or deletion request.
